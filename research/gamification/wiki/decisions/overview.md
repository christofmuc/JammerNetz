# Research decisions

Decisions connect accumulated evidence to implementation without allowing the
wiki to productize ideas automatically.

## Decision states

- `proposed`: drafted for discussion;
- `accepted`: confirmed by a human owner;
- `rejected`: considered and declined with a reason;
- `deferred`: blocked on an explicit condition;
- `superseded`: replaced, retaining history.

## Required content

- decision and owner;
- user or musical problem;
- alternatives considered;
- evidence and experiment IDs;
- trade-offs, risks, and uncertainty;
- production boundary and validation required;
- condition that would reopen the decision.

## Seed decisions awaiting evidence

- Prefer explicit DAW/drum-machine transport over audio beat tracking when
  available.
- Treat MIDI as symbolic intent and audio as realized sound.
- Separate personal perceived-clock timing from canonical ensemble timing.
- Use cooperative group progress and private coaching before public rankings.
- Begin with rhythm/onset replay before real-time harmonic scoring.

These are hypotheses, not accepted decisions, until entered through the
[decision template](../_templates/decision.md) and reviewed.
