# Grollmisch and Cano 2019

## What the source reports

The one-page demonstration paper integrates an extended chord recognizer into
the Songs2See Editor, which imports a recording and produces melody, beat, key,
chord, and printable score information.

## Interpretation

It demonstrates application integration and educational intent, not causal
live recognition or a reproducible model. The technical contribution is in the
related Nadar, Abesser, and Grollmisch paper.

## JammerNetz relevance

This is `reference` prior art for music-education presentation. It should not
be used as evidence for live latency or guitar robustness.
