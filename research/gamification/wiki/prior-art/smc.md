# Sound and Music Computing proceedings coverage

## Objective

Create a complete metadata inventory of SMC proceedings, screen it consistently,
then read only the relevant subset in full. Record discovery, screening,
full-text review, artifact audit, and reproduction as different states.

the first playable onset, timing, form, and feedback benchmarks come first.

## Relevant topic filters

- online or causal chord/harmony recognition;
- audio and performance-MIDI beat/downbeat tracking;
- onset and perceptual attack detection;
- score following and audio/MIDI alignment;
- guitar transcription and strumming;
- interaction, co-creation, education, and real-time feedback;
- networked performance, synchronization, and latency.
